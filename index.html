ASEM BASIL ABO RAHMA VIDEOGRAPHER

With over six years of professional experience behind the lens, I have mastered the art of capturing the pulse of a moment—whether it’s the high-stakes energy of a brand launch, the rhythmic precision of a commercial kitchen, or the intimate, unscripted emotions of a wedding.

My work sits at the intersection of cinematic artistry and strategic marketing. I’ve spent over half a decade collaborating with brands, restaurants, and fashion houses to create visual assets that don’t just look beautiful, but drive engagement and tell a story.

From the runway to the altar, and from global advertisements to local restaurant debuts, I bring a signature style defined by technical excellence, adaptive lighting, and intentional editing. I don't just document events—I curate the visual legacy of your brand or your most significant life milestones.
